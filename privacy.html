Privacy at GIFTCTRL

GIFTCTRL is a gift idea website, not a store. We do not currently ask visitors to create an account, enter payment information or provide contact information to use the gift quiz.

Information stored on your device

Your saved favorite gifts are stored in your browser using localStorage under the name giftctrl_faves. We do not operate a server that receives or stores these saved favorites. You can delete them by clearing the website's stored browser data.

Gift quiz

Your quiz answers are used in the page to rank gift ideas. They are not sent to us or stored in an account.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts for visual typography. Your browser may connect to Google to load fonts, subject to Google's own practices. Clicking a retailer search link takes you to a third-party website such as Target, Best Buy, Etsy, Sephora or Google, which has its own privacy practices and may use cookies.

Analytics and affiliate links

At launch, we have not installed a site analytics tracking script or affiliate tracking links. If that changes, this policy and any disclosures near monetized recommendations will be updated before those features go live.
