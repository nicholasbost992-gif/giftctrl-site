Terms & shopping transparency

Gift ideas are suggestions

GIFTCTRL provides editorial gift categories and a simple preference-based quiz for inspiration. Recommendations are not personal guarantees and are not independently tested reviews of any individual product listing.

We do not sell the products

Links on our site open external retailer searches so you can compare options. We do not handle checkout, deliveries, refunds or returns. Any transaction is directly with the retailer or seller you choose.

Prices and availability

Budget labels are approximate categories, not verified current prices. Product availability, features, shipping dates, taxes and prices may change. Check the actual seller and listing carefully before buying. We do not guarantee delivery before Christmas.

Monetization

At launch, our outbound links are non-affiliate retailer search links. We do not receive commissions from them. If paid relationships are added, we will disclose them clearly and close to the recommendations.
